Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you visit the avdcompany.com website operated by Avd Company and when you use our services. Your personal data is processed in accordance with Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data and the related legislation.

Information collected

Depending on how you use our website, the following types of information may be processed:

  • The name, e-mail address, telephone number and message content you send through contact forms
  • The IP address, browser and access records needed for website security and for obligations arising from the relevant legislation
  • Usage information obtained through cookies, depending on your preferences
  • The information required by the transaction if you place an order or request a service

Use and sharing of information

Your information is used to respond to your requests, to provide our services, to keep the website secure and working, and to fulfil our legal obligations. Your information is not sold; it is shared only with the service providers needed to deliver the service or with legally authorised institutions and organisations, to the extent permitted by the legislation.

Security and retention

We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss and misuse. Information is kept for the period required by the purpose of processing or prescribed by the legislation; at the end of that period it is deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

Your rights and contact

You may exercise your rights concerning your personal data within the framework of Law No. 6698. For detailed information you can review our Protection of Personal Data and Privacy Notice page, and you can send your questions via info@avdcompany.com.
